Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tiny Trouble VR ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our VR application Tiny Trouble VR (the "App") available on the Meta Quest Store.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All game data (scores, progress, settings) is stored locally on your device and never sent to our servers.

However, when you download and run the App through the Meta Quest Store, Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta") may collect certain information as governed by their own privacy policy. This includes:

  • Your Meta / Oculus account information
  • Basic usage analytics and crash logs (if you have opted in on your device)
  • Hardware / device identifiers

We do not receive any personally identifiable information from Meta — only aggregate, anonymized statistics when available.

2. Hand Tracking & Camera Data

The App uses the Meta Quest hand-tracking system to read hand and finger positions. All processing is performed entirely on-device in real time. No hand-tracking data, video frames, or depth sensor data is ever recorded, saved, or transmitted off your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or SDKs that collect data. The only third-party involved is:

  • Meta / Oculus Platform — provides the app distribution, entitlement checking, and optional leaderboard services. Their data handling is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable age of consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete data held by third parties (Meta). Since we do not hold any of your personal data ourselves, these requests should be directed to Meta.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
