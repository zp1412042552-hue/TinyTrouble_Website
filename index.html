About the Game

Tiny Trouble VR is a hand-tracking flight racing game built exclusively for Meta Quest. Transform your hands into wings and navigate through rings, dodge obstacles, and compete for the best time across increasingly challenging courses.

No controllers needed — just natural arm and hand movements let you fly, dive, and brake through a whimsical 3D world. With intuitive controls and progressive difficulty, anyone can pick it up and soar.

Features

✋ Full Hand Tracking

No controllers required. Fly using natural hand gestures — tilt to turn, dive to descend.

🏁 Race Courses

Multiple tracks with ring gates, obstacles, and hidden shortcuts to master.

🏆 Time Trials

Compete against your own best times. Perfect your route and climb the leaderboard.

🎯 Progressive Difficulty

Start with gentle slopes and wide rings, then tackle tight corridors and hazard fields.

🎨 Colorful World

A vibrant, stylized environment designed for comfort in VR.

📱 Quest Optimized

Built from the ground up for Meta Quest standalone — smooth 72 FPS.
