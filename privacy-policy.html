Last updated November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Tiny Trouble VR ("we", "our", or "us") processes information when you use the App on Meta Quest. It also applies to the store version named Tiny Trouble VR Demo. You can contact us at zp1412042552@gmail.com.

The developer operates this App and is responsible for the App-related personal data described here that is under our control. For deletion instructions, go directly to Requesting Deletion of Your Data.

1. Meta User ID and Account Identification

When Meta Platform features are enabled, the App uses Meta's Platform SDK and the identity of the signed-in Meta account, including the Meta User ID used by the platform, to perform account-specific purchase and entitlement checks. This identifies which account's purchases should be checked so that paid content is unlocked for the correct user and purchases can be restored. This account information comes from Meta, not from a separate account you create with us.

Our current purchase integration requests the signed-in account's purchases through Meta's account-scoped APIs. It does not separately retrieve, log, or save the raw Meta User ID in a developer-operated database or gameplay save file. Processing through Meta's SDK still involves account-related user data, even when we do not maintain our own server copy. We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

The source of account identification is the Meta account currently signed in on the headset. Meta's account-scoped SDK associates the App's purchase requests with that account. The App uses the resulting account-specific ownership status to decide whether to allow access to purchased worlds. User ID access supports this purchase-verification purpose. The current integration does not request a separate profile, email address, friends list, or contact list from Meta.

2. In-App Purchases and Downloadable Content

When purchase features are enabled, the App receives and processes product information, product identifiers (SKUs), localized prices, purchase-query results, checkout success or failure, and content ownership or entitlement status through the Meta Platform SDK. The current expansion product is tt_premium_worlds, a non-consumable purchase for the Tiny Trouble and Alley worlds. Availability of paid features can differ between App versions; a version with purchases disabled does not run this purchase flow.

Purchase data processing from request to access

  • Product lookup. The App sends the expansion SKU to Meta's product service and reads the matching SKU and formatted local price from the response to display the offer.
  • Ownership lookup. Meta returns purchase records for the signed-in account. The SDK response may contain platform purchase identifiers and other transaction fields. The App's current ownership logic reads the purchase SKU and pagination information, compares the SKU with tt_premium_worlds, and keeps the resulting owned or unowned status in session memory.
  • Checkout. Choosing Purchase sends the SKU to Meta's checkout service. The App processes the completion or error result and then queries ownership again. Access is granted when ownership is verified.
  • Restoration and offline checks. The App queries purchases again when restoring access or returning to the foreground. When an online query fails, it can request Meta's on-device durable-purchase cache. This uses the ownership information associated with the platform account.

SDK response messages are released after processing. The App keeps the displayed price and ownership state in memory for the session. It does not create a developer-side account ledger, transaction history, or purchase database. The expansion is a durable, non-consumable entitlement, so the App does not deduct dialogue credits or consume this purchase after use. Meta continues to maintain its own purchase history and platform cache according to its policies.

  • We retrieve product information to display the available expansion and its current localized price.
  • We launch Meta's checkout interface when you choose to purchase.
  • We check account-specific purchases to verify ownership, unlock content, restore purchases, and avoid granting access to an account that does not own the product.
  • After checkout and when the App returns to the foreground, the App can recheck ownership. If an online check fails, it can read Meta's on-device durable-purchase cache for offline access.

Ownership state is held in memory during the App session; we do not write a separate ownership flag into gameplay saves or send purchase results to a developer-operated purchase server. Meta manages the payment and purchase records. We do not receive your payment-card number, payment credentials, or Meta password. Diagnostic messages about purchase requests contain request/error codes or ownership/cache status, rather than raw User IDs, payment details, or full purchase records.

3. Local Gameplay, Settings, and Hand Tracking

The App saves gameplay progress, scores and best times, tutorial completion, in-game inventory and world progress, and settings such as sound preferences locally on your device. These records allow you to continue playing and keep your preferences between sessions. The current App does not upload these gameplay saves to a developer-operated server.

Meta Quest provides hand and finger pose and movement information for flight, gesture recognition, and menu interaction. The App processes these inputs on-device. It does not transmit hand-tracking data to us or access or upload raw camera images or video for these controls. Where gesture recording or calibration tools are available and explicitly activated, derived hand-pose templates can be saved in a local gesture profile. These profiles are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

4. Support Communications

If you email us for support or to exercise a privacy right, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as an App version, problem description, or account identifier needed to locate relevant records. We use this information to respond, resolve the issue, verify a request when necessary, and carry out data requests. Please do not send passwords or payment-card information.

5. Sharing and Meta Platform Services

The App communicates with Meta through the Platform SDK to initialize platform services, retrieve products and account-specific purchases, and complete checkout. We do not sell user data or share Meta User IDs or purchase information with advertising networks. Email support is processed through our email service provider. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

  • Meta. The Meta Platform SDK and Meta services process account identification, product requests, checkout, and purchase verification for the App. Meta controls its account, payment, and platform transaction records.
  • Google Gmail. Our support address uses Gmail. If you contact us, Google processes the email and its delivery information as part of providing email services. We use the correspondence to handle your support or privacy request.
  • GitHub Pages. This public policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening the website involves normal web requests to GitHub, which may process your IP address and technical request information under its privacy practices.

The current App has no developer-operated purchase or gameplay backend and includes no developer-integrated advertising or analytics service. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable privacy terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Meta independently processes information for account, store, payment, device, and platform services under Meta's Privacy Policy. Meta may separately collect platform diagnostics or provide aggregate store statistics. Meta-controlled records are distinct from any records we hold.

6. Retention and Security

  • Account-scoped purchase responses, price, and ownership state. The App processes SDK responses during purchase checks and retains its working state in session memory. Meta manages any persistent platform purchase cache.
  • Gameplay saves and optional gesture profiles. These persist locally so progress, preferences, and recorded control profiles remain available between sessions, until you clear the App's data or delete the relevant files.
  • Diagnostics. The App can write local technical logs, including purchase request/error codes and ownership/cache status. Their lifetime depends on the device and App logging controls. They can be removed with local App data. If you send logs to support, the submitted copy is handled as support correspondence.
  • Support and privacy requests. We keep correspondence while needed to resolve the issue, carry out the request, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request its deletion at any time. Any limited record retained to document compliance or defend a legal claim is restricted to that purpose.

Local saves and any locally saved gesture profiles remain until removed through device app-data controls or otherwise deleted. Platform-managed purchase caches and any platform backup copies follow Meta's controls and retention practices. We retain support communications only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. We restrict access to developer-held information and use the platform SDK for purchase handling rather than collecting payment credentials ourselves.

Access to developer-held support correspondence is limited to people who need it to handle the request. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information involved. Transmission and storage services also rely on the protections provided by Meta and our email provider.

7. Requesting Deletion of Your Data

All users, regardless of country or geographic location, may request deletion of their App-related personal data held by us, free of charge. You do not need to purchase content to make a request.

To request deletion, email zp1412042552@gmail.com with the subject "Tiny Trouble VR Data Deletion Request". Tell us that you want your App-related data deleted. Include only enough information to locate the records concerned, such as the email address used for earlier support correspondence. If an account identifier is necessary, we will explain what is needed and why; do not send your Meta password or payment-card details.

We will review the request, request proportionate identity verification only where needed, delete the personal data we hold or control, and confirm the result without undue delay and within applicable legal deadlines. If we do not hold server-side records for your account, we will explain that and help you identify the relevant local or platform controls. We may retain only information required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims; if this applies, we will explain the reason and scope of the retention in our response.

What happens after you send a request

  • We identify the App-related records under our control, including support emails and any attachments you previously supplied. You may request deletion of all such records or specify particular records.
  • Where verification is necessary, we ask for the minimum information reasonably needed. We do not require payment or a new developer account to process a deletion request.
  • We delete the relevant developer-controlled records and copies under our control, subject to the limited legal exceptions explained above, and tell you what was deleted.
  • If any requested data must be retained, we explain which data, the reason, and the applicable retention requirement. You can reply to the same email address to ask us to reconsider or clarify the result.
  • If the information exists only on your headset or is independently controlled by Meta, we explain the relevant device or platform steps. We still handle any developer-controlled information included in the same request.

You can also send a deletion request using your own email application if the email link does not open. Send it to zp1412042552@gmail.com and name Tiny Trouble VR or Tiny Trouble VR Demo. A support-correspondence deletion request usually needs only the email address used for that correspondence.

To remove gameplay saves, settings, and any saved gesture profiles from your headset, use the device's clear-app-data or storage controls for Tiny Trouble VR. This resets local progress. If Meta cloud backup is enabled for your App data, manage the backup through Meta's backup controls as well so that old data is not restored later. You may contact us at the same email address for help with these steps.

Deleting our records or clearing local App data does not delete your Meta account, cancel a purchase, or erase transaction records independently held by Meta. Meta controls those records, and requests concerning them can be made through Meta's account/privacy controls. We will help direct you to those controls when relevant; this does not replace your right to request deletion of data held by us. If you use the purchase features again, Meta may provide your current entitlement status again to restore owned content.
