Tiny Trouble VR Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy applies to Tiny Trouble VR on the Meta Quest platform, including the version formerly named Tiny Trouble VR Demo.

1. Core Principle: We Do Not Collect Any User Information

We are committed to protecting user privacy. The App has no developer-operated server. We do not and cannot collect or upload any of your personal information.

  • No personal data. We do not separately obtain or store your raw Meta User ID. We also do not retain copies of any of your purchase records, Meta passwords, payment-card numbers, or payment credentials.
  • No data misuse. No data is used for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

2. Paid Levels and Purchase Verification

The App includes multiple levels. Currently, the Tiny Trouble and Alley levels require a purchase to unlock.

  • Users purchase an expansion through Meta's official channels to unlock these two levels. All payments, transactions, and account identification are managed entirely by the Meta platform.
  • The App only reads product prices and purchase results locally through the Meta SDK to verify and restore your purchased levels on the headset.
  • Purchase verification results are held temporarily in your headset's runtime memory. Local logs may record verification status and error codes. This data is never uploaded to us.

3. Local Game Data and Hand Tracking

All game-related data exists only on your local device. We never receive it.

  • Game data. Your game saves, scores, game settings, and gesture profiles are all stored on your own headset.
  • Hand tracking. The App uses on-device hand and finger position information provided by Meta Quest for flight and interaction. All processing takes place locally on the headset. We never collect or upload your hand-tracking data or raw camera images.

4. Third-Party Services

The App relies solely on the official Meta platform for app distribution, account identification, payments, and purchase verification. The processing of related platform information is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy. The App does not integrate any third-party advertising or analytics plugins.

5. Data Deletion (We Hold No User Data)

Because we do not hold any of your game saves, raw Meta User ID, or purchase records, there are no records of your data on the developer's side to delete.

  • Local headset data. Game saves, settings, gesture profiles, and logs are stored on your headset. To remove them, use the headset's built-in app storage management controls.
  • Meta platform data. Requests concerning account and transaction records managed by Meta must be made through Meta's official account or privacy controls.

6. Policy Updates

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will publish the changes on this page and update the date above.
